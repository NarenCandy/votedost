Election Guide

Essential information about the Indian electoral process.

🏛️

What is ECI

The Election Commission of India is an autonomous constitutional authority responsible for administering election processes in India.

✅

Voter Eligibility

You must be an Indian citizen, 18 years or older as of January 1st, and registered as a voter in your constituency.

🪪

How to get Voter ID

Fill Form 6 online at nvsp.in or offline with address and age proof to register and obtain your Voter ID card.

👨‍💼

Candidate Eligibility

Must be an Indian citizen, at least 25 years old (for Lok Sabha), a registered voter, and have no serious criminal convictions.

⚖️

Model Code of Conduct

Guidelines issued by ECI for political parties and candidates during elections to maintain a level playing field and fair practice.

📠

What is EVM

Electronic Voting Machines are used to record votes electronically, ensuring a secure, transparent, and quick polling process.

🧾

What is VVPAT

Voter Verifiable Paper Audit Trail allows voters to verify that their vote was cast correctly by printing a paper slip.

❌

Right to NOTA

None Of The Above is an option allowing voters to officially register a vote of rejection for all contesting candidates.

Results & Declaration 🏆

Winners declared, new government formation begins

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about voting in India.

How do I register to vote? +

Visit the National Voters Service Portal (nvsp.in) or your nearest Electoral Registration Office. Fill Form 6 with your Aadhaar, age proof, and address proof. You can also register through the Voter Helpline App.

What ID can I use at the polling booth? +

While Voter ID (EPIC) is primary, ECI also accepts Aadhaar card, PAN card, Passport, Driving License, MNREGA Job Card, bank/post office passbook with photo, or any government-issued photo ID.

What is NOTA? +

NOTA stands for None of the Above. If you find no candidate suitable, you can press the NOTA button on the EVM. Your vote is counted but does not go to any candidate.

Can I vote if my name is not on the voter list? +

No. Your name must be on the electoral roll of your constituency. You can check at electoralsearch.eci.gov.in or the Voter Helpline App.

What is the minimum age to vote? +

18 years as of January 1st of the year the electoral roll is revised.

Can I vote from any booth? +

No. You must vote at the specific polling booth assigned to your address as per the voter list.

What happens if someone offers me money for my vote? +

It is illegal. Report it immediately to the ECI via the cVIGIL app or call 1950 (Voter Helpline).

What is the difference between Lok Sabha and Vidhan Sabha elections? +

Lok Sabha is the national parliament election held every 5 years. Vidhan Sabha is the state legislative assembly election. Both use the same EVM-based voting process.

How do I check my name on the voter list? +

Visit electoralsearch.eci.gov.in, enter your details, and check your registration status.

What is Model Code of Conduct? +

MCC is a set of guidelines by ECI that political parties and candidates must follow from election announcement until results. It ensures free and fair elections.
